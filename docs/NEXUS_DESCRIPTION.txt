[b][size=5]Callisto SSS — Callisto BRDF & SSS kernel[/size][/b]
[b]Linux / Steam Play (Proton) only · v1.0.0[/b]

Vanilla Cyberpunk's path tracer shades skin with a plain Lambert diffuse (albedo/π) plus GGX specular — [i]no skin-specific lobe at all[/i]. That's a big part of why faces can look dry, matte and gravelly, especially under frontal lighting.

This mod makes two surgical, composable changes:

[b]1. Callisto BRDF injection (the main event)[/b]
A small Vulkan layer hot-swaps the path tracer's ray-generation shader at load time with a patched version that adds the Callisto [b]c1[/b] terms (from [i]"The Character Rendering Art of 'The Callisto Protocol'"[/i], SIGGRAPH 2023):
[list]
[*][b]Diffuse Fresnel[/b] — soft, fleshy response at grazing angles instead of the flat Lambert falloff
[*][b]Retroreflection[/b] — the subtle front-lit glow that makes skin look alive
[/list]
The patch is [b]skin-gated[/b]: it only touches pixels the engine itself classifies as skin (the exact test the game's own SSS pass uses). Every other material renders bit-identically to vanilla.

[b]2. Callisto-reshaped SSS blur kernel[/b]
A RED4ext plugin replaces the engine's runtime-generated subsurface diffusion kernel with a reshaped profile (wider red-channel bleed, softened center). Toggleable in-game via the CET overlay.

Ships pre-tuned to tasteful defaults — no configuration required.

[b][size=4]FAQ[/size][/b]
[b]Windows?[/b] No. The BRDF patch relies on Proton translating shaders to SPIR-V (where a Vulkan layer can substitute them); on native Windows the mechanism doesn't exist.
[b]Without Path Tracing?[/b] The BRDF patch is PT-only; the SSS kernel applies in all modes.
[b]Performance?[/b] Effectively free — two shaders substituted at load, one small texture patched at boot.
[b]Conflicts?[/b] No game files are modified; composes with other mods.
[b]Game updates?[/b] If CDPR recompiles the PT shaders the layer passes through (vanilla look, no crash) until the mod updates.
